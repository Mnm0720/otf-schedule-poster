What it reads from the post

The threads describe a month by category, not day by day, so the schedule is assembled by inverting those lists. Four things are read; everything else is ignored.

Welcome to the September 2026 Monthly Thread!
... for September it is Rhythm & Routine.

Key Dates for The Month
* September 8 (Tuesday): 1000 Meter Row; benchmark. ...
* August 1 (Saturday) - August 31 (Monday): Marathon Month; event. ...

* Run/Rows on 9/3, 9/5, 9/8 (benchmark), 9/13, ...
* Repeat templates are as follows: 9/16 = 9/1, 9/17 = 9/2, ...

Any day no category names becomes a Standard template. The (benchmark) hints cross-reference Key Dates and don't add a second pill. A plain day list (9/1 - Standard) is also accepted and detected automatically.

Unknown workout types are kept on their dates and receive a new color. Check spelling; if it is a new type, let the developer know so it can become a default.

Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Orangetheory Fitness. Schedule data comes from the r/orangetheory monthly threads.
