      Help: make, edit and share a poster
      1. Start: copy the monthly Reddit post, including its title and workout lists. Paste it above and choose Generate poster.
      2. Review: check the detected month. Open Review all dates and follow any Check date buttons to fix problems.
      3. Edit: choose a date or use Previous and Next. Optional sections change the title, colors, notes and credits.
      4. Update: choose Update preview after editing, then Preview and download. Previous/Next version lets you revisit earlier work and recover pending edits.
      5. Download: choose your format and Download. On a phone, open the file from your browser downloads or use the Open file link.
      6. Recover or hand off: use Saved Drafts to reopen work in this browser. Open Save, back up or share for a draft file or editable copy link. Send a new copy back after editing; copies do not sync.
      Advanced: supported thread text

      The threads describe a month by category, not day by day, so the schedule is assembled by inverting those lists. Four things are read; everything else is ignored.

      Welcome to the September 2026 Monthly Thread!
... for September it is Rhythm & Routine.

Key Dates for The Month
* September 8 (Tuesday): 1000 Meter Row; benchmark. ...
* August 1 (Saturday) - August 31 (Monday): Marathon Month; event. ...

* Run/Rows on 9/3, 9/5, 9/8 (benchmark), 9/13, ...
* Repeat templates are as follows: 9/16 = 9/1, 9/17 = 9/2, ...

      Any day no category names becomes a Standard template. The (benchmark) hints cross-reference Key Dates and don't add a second pill. A plain day list (9/1 - Standard) is also accepted and detected automatically.

      Unknown workout types are kept on their dates and receive a new color. Check spelling; if it is a new type, let the developer know so it can become a default.

      Restart from text?

      This will remove all customizations from the current editing session, including changes already applied to the poster. Your saved draft and original text will be kept so you can return to them. Generate again to start a separate fresh poster.
